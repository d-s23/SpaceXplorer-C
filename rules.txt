Welcome to SpaceXplorer!

You are the captain of a spaceship on a mission to make your way back to Earth. On your way you will explore
different planets, collect resources like fuel and space junk to help you on your way to locate Earth.

The universe is dangerous, so you will need to manage your resources wisely and avoid running out of fuel or
damaging your ship!

Game Rules:
1. You start with a limited amount of fuel and ship health.
2. Each lightspeed you make consumes fuel. Be sure to keep an eye on your fuel levels!
3. You can collect fuel and space junk from planets you visit.
4. Use fuel to keep moving, and space junk can be used for repairs.
5. If you run out of fuel, or your ship's health reaches 0, the game will end as well.
6. The goal is to find Earth. Keep exploring, and with each step, your chances of finding Earth increase!
7. You can only repair your ship when you're on a planet.
8. There will be things like a meteors and potentially aliens looking to attack you so watch out!
